# Round 2 revision brief (owner direction, 2026-11-17)

## 1. Restructure: nutmeg is the main character
The owner: "Rather than making it about Run and Manhattan, make it about the nutmeg. How one seed was responsible for
deaths, how it ended up on a flag, and how it was traded for Manhattan."

- **Hook framing:** a seed you grate onto eggnog (harmless in kitchen amounts; see facts2 on toxicity) that emptied an
  island chain. The death toll is "an entire people": of every 15 Bandanese, 1 remained (about 15,000 → about 1,000).
  NEVER "millions" (owner chose this framing).
- **Whole-video question,** roughly: *Why would anyone kill for a seed, and how did it end up on a national flag and
  tangled up with Manhattan?*
- **"There are five reasons"** (the owner wants "reasons", never "steps"). Roughly:
  1. The seed: it grew in only one place.
  2. The route: it multiplied in price on the way to Europe.
  3. The company: the VOC wanted all of it.
  4. The island: Run, the English, the siege, then 1621.
  5. The treaty: Breda, Run and Manhattan.
  - **Payoff:** the seed escapes (Poivre, then Britain), reaches Grenada and goes onto its flag.
  - **Close:** back to the kitchen.
- The reason names must work as 1–2-word labels under the top-right icon bar (e.g. THE SEED, THE ROUTE, THE COMPANY,
  THE ISLAND, THE TREATY).
