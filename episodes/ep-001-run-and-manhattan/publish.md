**Title (pick one):**
1. The Tiny Island the Dutch Traded for Manhattan
2. Why the Dutch Kept a Speck and Let Manhattan Go
3. The Only Island Where Nutmeg Grew

**Description:**
In 1667 the Dutch "traded" Manhattan for Run, a nutmeg island about the size of Central Park. Except they didn't. This is the story of the nutmeg route, the siege of Run, the conquest of the Banda Islands in 1621, and the treaty that let the Dutch close the last hole in their monopoly, until the seeds got out.
